## Boundaries and next work

This phase executes local simulator operations through APIs and a deterministic development helper. It does not implement the full response-editor/browser execution workflow (PH-05), autonomous AI orchestration (PH-06), or review issuance/resume workflow (PH-07). Required review is already enforced: review-required sends wait unless matching, current persisted approval exists. Unsupported taxonomy, production document mappings and policy gaps remain explicit.

The Azure probe demonstrates connectivity, a function call, scoped dispatcher access and use of its result. It does not establish autonomous planning, all tool-schema capabilities or successful completion of the future AI workflow.
