## Boundaries and next work

Assessment results are recommendations and preflight checks. They do not execute routing, create tasks, update a name, send correspondence, index a package, write an ILS note, approve a response or close a case. The positive completion test constructs matching persisted simulator records within an isolated test database; it does not claim that the simulator workflow has been implemented.

Only the selected scenario rules are configured. Unsupported operational mappings and production policy gaps remain visible. The next-review convention is the next Eastern weekday at the same local time, recorded as a demo convention. Typed case intake rejects malformed timestamps; the reusable routing service also handles invalid timestamps explicitly for future ingestion adapters.

The response checker accepts its controlled rendering of structured claims and rejects additional prose; arbitrary natural-language validation and the full response editor remain later-phase work. Repetitive responses, no-response exceptions, C&D reversal execution and production disclosures have no newly invented approvals.
