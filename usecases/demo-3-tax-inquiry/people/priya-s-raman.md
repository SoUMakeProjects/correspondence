## Background

Priya is 31 and works as a clinical research coordinator. In April 2026 she and her partner bought their first home, a three-bedroom house on Hawthorne Ridge Road in Wrenfield Township (Juniper County, Pennsylvania). The loan is in her name. Property taxes and homeowners insurance are paid from an escrow account, and that was explained at closing. But the title company paid the third-quarter tax installment at closing, so the fourth-quarter bill is the first one the servicer is supposed to pay.

In mid-September her copy of the township's real estate tax bill arrives in the mail. It says a copy was sent to her mortgage company, but it also shows a due date six weeks away and a 10% penalty if payment is late. She is careful with money and doesn't want to find out in November that nobody paid it. On Saturday morning she emails Harbor Point with a photo-quality scan of the bill attached.
