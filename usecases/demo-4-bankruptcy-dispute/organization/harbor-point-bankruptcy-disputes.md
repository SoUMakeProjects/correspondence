## How Harbor Point handles a credit reporting dispute that depends on bankruptcy status

1. **Route it as a dispute.** An alleged credit reporting error goes to *Customer Upset / Compliance* (RULE-04), whatever its age. The correspondence desk does not investigate or decide it.
2. **Honor the representation.** When the borrower is represented and has signed an authorization, every communication about the loan goes to counsel at the verified address. Nothing goes to the borrower's own address, not even a copy.
3. **Do not resolve conflicting bankruptcy records at the desk.** A court order and a servicing marker that disagree are a conflict for the Bankruptcy Team. *Dismissed* and *discharged* are not interchangeable. Reuse the existing Bankruptcy Team task for the case; never open a duplicate.
4. **Acknowledge the dispute in writing, once.** Send counsel an acknowledgment that:
   - confirms the date the dispute was received and the documents that came with it;
   - confirms counsel-only contact;
   - says the dispute has been referred to the bankruptcy and credit reporting specialists;
   - says plainly that it is **not** the result of the investigation, and makes no statement about how the account is or will be reported, about corrections or about liability;
   - gives the date by which a written response will be sent (30 days from receipt);
   - attaches nothing: counsel's own documents are not sent back, and the Bankruptcy Team's memo is internal.

   The acknowledgment is reviewed like every Harbor Point letter. It is the only letter the correspondence desk sends on the case; after it, the written response belongs to Compliance.
5. **Record the handoff with a routing note** that the receiving team can act on without opening the file: loan, borrower and receipt date; the reason for the route; what the court order and the servicing marker each say (or the Bankruptcy Team determination, once made); the contact restriction; the acknowledgment delivery and the response due date; the open items; and what still blocks closure.
6. **When the Bankruptcy Team result arrives**, the earlier handoff is out of date. Reassess, and record a current handoff that cites the determination. A determination only corrects the *servicing* record. It does not decide the credit reporting dispute.
7. **Transfer, don't close.** Compliance acknowledges the current handoff, and the case becomes *transferred*. It stays open: the concerns remain tracked, and there is still no approved classification mapping for this request (GAP-08).

## Brand usage in this demo

The acknowledgment is addressed to counsel by name and signed by the Customer Care Team. It omits the "Thank you for choosing Harbor Point" line used in letters to customers, because it goes to the borrower's attorney, not to the customer. It ends with a reference block and the Harbor Point notice. The court order and the authorization are third-party documents. The Bankruptcy Team memo is an internal record marked *not for release*.
