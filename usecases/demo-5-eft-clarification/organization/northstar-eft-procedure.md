# Northstar Residential Servicing: electronic transfer requests

Client details (sender, Customer Care, address, notice) are in [Demo 1's organization profile](../../demo-1-amortization/organization/northstar-residential-servicing.md). Northstar uses **automatic** review mode, so these letters are sent without a human approval step.

## Three different requests behind "EFT"

| What the borrower means | What Northstar needs before anything moves |
|---|---|
| **A refund sent to the borrower** (for example an escrow surplus refund) by direct deposit instead of a check | A signed and dated **Electronic Refund Authorization** giving the account holder, bank, account type, and routing and account numbers, and authorizing the deposit; plus a **voided check or bank letter** for that account |
| **Automatic monthly payments** (the borrower's payment drawn from their bank each month) | A signed and dated **automatic payment (ACH debit) authorization** with the same bank details; plus a **voided check or bank letter**. The borrower keeps paying as usual until the start date is confirmed in writing |
| **A draw from a home equity line of credit** sent to the borrower's bank | A signed and dated **draw request** naming the line of credit, the amount and the receiving account; plus a **voided check or bank letter**. A fixed-rate mortgage has no line to draw from, so the borrower must identify the line |

## How Northstar handles the request

1. **Identify** the requester against the loan record: the sender must be the borrower of record and the authorized recipient, and there must be no communication restrictions.
2. **Don't guess.** If the email does not say which of the three it means, send a letter that explains all three in plain words and asks which one. Change nothing.
3. **Once the purpose is known**, ask only for that purpose's authorization and bank proof. Say how to send them securely (attachments or mail, never the account number typed into an email), and that **no money will move until both are received and verified**.
4. **Wait for the borrower.** The next step is theirs. This is not a specialist handoff while contact is permitted. If contact is blocked (for example, cease and desist), record a handoff instead of writing.
5. **Record every letter**: send from the client address, index the package in OnBase as *INQ Email Reply*, and add an ILS Standout Comment with the delivery reference.

The demo has **no funds-movement tool** at all. Even with a complete authorization, moving money would be a separate, human-controlled servicing action.
