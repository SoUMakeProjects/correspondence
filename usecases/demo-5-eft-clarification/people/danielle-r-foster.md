## Background

Danielle is 46 and a high-school chemistry teacher. She refinanced the Birchwood Court house into a 15-year fixed loan in June 2019 and has paid by mailed check ever since. That is partly habit, and partly because she never got around to setting anything else up.

In September her annual escrow statement arrives. Her county tax bill went down, so the account has a **$486.27 surplus** that will be refunded **by check**, and her monthly payment drops to **$1,562.96** from November. The last escrow refund check sat on her desk for two weeks and then took days to clear. She writes a short email: "Can you change this to an EFT instead?" She means the refund, but "this" could just as well be her monthly payment, which the same statement talks about. The servicer has to ask.

## What a good outcome looks like to Danielle

A quick, plain question that doesn't make her feel she did something wrong. Then a short list of exactly what to send and how to send it safely. She also wants a clear statement that nothing happens until the documents arrive, so she isn't surprised by a transfer she didn't authorize, or by a missing refund.
